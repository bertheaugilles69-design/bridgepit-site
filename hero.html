Setting it up

Four screens, and a test fill before any money moves.

No file to edit, no terminal, nothing to paste into a config. Every step ends in something you can see for yourself.

1

Open it and pick a password.

There is no account to create and nothing to sign into. The password guards the dashboard on this machine, and it is stored here.

You know it workedThe app opens without a warning from macOS, because Apple has signed and notarised it. If you forget the password there is no reset link — by design.

Straight answers

The questions people actually ask.

Can you see my broker login?

No. It is encrypted on your machine and never sent anywhere. We run no server in the path between you and your broker, so there is nothing on our side to leak.

Is this allowed where I trade?

Rules differ by firm and they change. BridgePit places orders from your own machine and your own connection, and you stay at the controls. Read your firm's rules — we never claim approval on your behalf.

What if my Mac closes mid-trade?

Your emergency stop is resting at the broker, not inside the app, so it still protects the position. The app also keeps the Mac awake while it is armed and warns you on battery.

Which brokers work today?

Rithmic. More are added only once each one is proven end to end with real orders, not before.

Does it trade on its own?

It acts on the alerts you set up, on the instruments and sizes you set, and it stops the moment you press halt. It is your strategy and your session — the app is the cockpit, not the pilot.

Windows?

macOS today, on Apple Silicon. The Windows build exists and is not released yet.
