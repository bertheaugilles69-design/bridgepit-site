The controls around every session

Set your limits.
See where you stand.

01

Your stop, at the broker.

When you configure a safety stop, BridgePit places it with the broker and checks that it is resting.

02

Each account’s own rules.

Set size, stop distance and loss limits for each account. Keep funded accounts and your own brokerage rules separate.

03

A deliberate stop.

Halt blocks new entries while exits remain allowed. Close all positions requests a close and checks the result.

04

Your closing time.

Choose an end-of-day close. BridgePit retries until the broker confirms you are flat, and raises an alarm if it cannot.

BridgePit needs to stay open on an awake, connected Mac. Closing the lid sleeps the computer. See how setup works ↗
