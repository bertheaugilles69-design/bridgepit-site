When it goes wrong

Four ways this goes wrong. Four things already in place.

Your internet drops

You’re long two, and the Wi-Fi dies.

Your stop is not a plan on some server. It is a live order resting at your broker — it works with your laptop unplugged.

Your laptop dies

The machine shuts off mid-trade.

Everything on screen goes with it. The one thing that matters doesn’t: the stop is already at the broker, and it needs neither us nor you.

Your alert misfires

The same alert fires three times.

One order goes. The next two are refused inside the same second, so a bad alert can’t quietly leave you long six.

You forget the close

It’s 15:55 and you walked away.

It flattens for you before the firm’s cutoff — and keeps asking until the broker confirms you are flat.
