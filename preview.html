Ours
Your login is typed into your own computer.

It is encrypted on your disk and used from there. It is never sent to us, because there is no server of ours in the path. We can’t lose what we never receive.

Your orders also carry the address you log in from, not one shared with every other customer.

Questions

Is this allowed at my prop firm?

Rules differ by firm and change, and some firms prohibit automation outright — check your firm’s current policy yourself. What we can tell you is what happens technically: the orders are placed by software on your own machine, using your own login, from your own address.

What if my laptop dies mid-trade?

The protective stop is a real order already resting at your broker, so it stays live without us and without you. New orders stop until the machine is back.

Do you ever see my broker password?

No. It is encrypted on your own disk and used from there. There is no server of ours in the path.

What happens if the same alert fires three times?

One order goes. The repeats are refused, so a noisy alert can’t quietly multiply your position.

Paper mode shows simulated fills, not a performance claim. You supervise; the software executes. Futures trading involves substantial risk of loss.
