Open the updated app

  1. In your running BridgePit, open Settings, scroll to the bottom and choose Quit BridgePit. Confirm, then close its old browser tabs.
  2. Open the downloaded installer. Drag BridgePit into Applications and replace the older app. Launch it from Applications and reload the dashboard.
  3. Check http://127.0.0.1:8787/api/version shows 1.4.23. If you deliberately use another port, use that port instead.
Confirmation places no order

Set up your strategy alert

  1. Open Settings → Alert setup, select your saved primary strategy and choose Supplied 14/28 SMA strategy · automatic confirmation. Choose Copy strategy.
  2. Save it as a new private Pine script in TradingView and add it to your usual chart. Keep the code private: it includes your alert token. Use 1 contract in TradingView; set your account size in BridgePit.
  3. Pause the old alert before replacing it. Create one alert with Order fills and alert() function calls. Keep the prefilled Message. Under Notifications, enable Webhook URL and paste your address using BridgePit’s Copy webhook URL.
  4. Keep BridgePit and Tailscale running. Return to Alert setup for Setup confirmation received and its time. A closed market or notification schedule can delay delivery.

This complete script is for the supplied 14/28 SMA crossover only. Other strategies keep their existing alert-message setup.

Then check the first paper trade

A new, never-filled BridgePit strategy stays flat until a real order alert arrives. The supplied alert includes its before-and-after positions, so BridgePit opens the intended position at your saved size without closing a historical trade it never held.

If the strategy already traded or was closed manually, check that its BridgePit and TradingView positions match before continuing. Verify actual paper fills and sizing. Remain in broker-free paper mode for this walkthrough.
